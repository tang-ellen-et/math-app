    1.[5]Call a 9-digit number a cassowary if it uses each of the digits 1 through 9 exactly once. Compute
the number of cassowaries that are prime.

Answer: 0
Solution: Every cassowary is divisible by 3, as the sum of its digits is 1 + 2 + ···+ 9 = 45. Since
all such numbers are divisible by 3 and and greater than 3, none of them are prime. So, there are 0
prime cassowaries.
