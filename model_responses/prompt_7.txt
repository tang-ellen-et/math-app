    7.[6]The number

1001

Proposed by: Derek Liu
Answer: 231
Solution: Observe
99−88= 276−166
= (272−162)(274+ 272·162+ 164)
= (27 −16)(27 + 16)(272−27·16 + 162)(272+ 27·16 + 162)
= 11·43·553·1417.
The remaining factorizations are motivated by the fact that 1001 = 7 ·11·13. We see that 553 = 7 ·79
and 1417 = 13 ·109, so the answer is 43 + 79 + 109 = 231 .
